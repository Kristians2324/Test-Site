Flour & Fermentation

Our country and seeded loaves ferment for 32 to 36 hours in cool proofing rooms using stone-ground Red Fife and Sonora wheat. Loaves come out of the deck oven between 7:30 AM and 9:00 AM.

Same-Day Counter Pickup

Pre-orders placed online are held at the side counter until 3:30 PM. Need to adjust your pickup time or add slicing instructions? Leave a note when checking out.
