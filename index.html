INSIDE THE KITCHEN

How We Build Every Bowl

No shortcuts, concentrates, or pre-made noodles—everything is prepped in-house from raw ingredients.

01

The 18-Hour Broth

Our stock kettles simmer continuously for 18 hours using pasture-raised pork femurs, roasted chicken frames, dried kelp, and shiitake caps for deep natural umami.

02

Custom Noodle Room

We blend high-protein hard red spring wheat with kansui mineral salts to roll springy, toothsome noodles tailored to hold up in rich hot broth.

03

Aged Tare & Aroma Oils

Each bowl starts with our 30-day aged shoyu or roasted white miso tare, finished with house-infused charred garlic mayu or Sichuan chili crisp.

04

Plant-Based Options

Our Creamy Sesame Mushroom Ramen uses a velvety cashew, roasted tahini, and kombu broth that rivals traditional pork tonkotsu in richness.
